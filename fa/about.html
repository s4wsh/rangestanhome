About Rangestan

Respectful painting, thoughtful art direction.

Rangestan began with friends repainting damp apartments. Today we are a structured studio with precise planning and calm crews.

Story

We saw families forced to choose between quick but messy painting or artistic services with long waitlists. Rangestan exists to bridge that gap with technical preparation, color knowledge, and respectful behavior.

Our job is done when you love your walls. That means clean tools, covered floors, clear pricing, and a handover kit that explains how to care for every finish.

Process

Step-by-step delivery.

Visit

We review WhatsApp photos and complete a focused site visit with moisture checks.

Planning

Scopes cover repair, paint specs, budget, and agreed schedule.

Execution

Crews work room-by-room with full protection and daily updates.

Check-up

We guide you through the result, leave a care note, and stay available.

Principles

  • Plan every project around your lifestyle.
  • Repair and prime thoroughly before paint.
  • Communicate through WhatsApp for clarity.
  • Leave each space cleaner than we found it.

What clients notice

Calm behavior, tidy rooms, and finishes that stay beautiful after we leave. That is the Rangestan standard.
