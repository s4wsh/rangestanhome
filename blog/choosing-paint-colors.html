Choosing the Right Paint Colors for Your Space

The perfect palette respects light, furniture, and how you want to feel inside the room. Use these checkpoints before locking a color.

Read the light

Stand in each room three times per day. Cooler daylight needs warmer tones to feel balanced; bright evening light allows muted neutrals. Light bulbs also push colors toward blue or yellow, so note the fixtures.

Define the mood

Gather three or four reference photos and add words to them: calm, bold, minimal, playful. Those words translate into saturation and contrast choices when we prepare sample boards.

Sample large areas

Never rely on tiny swatches. Paint panels or test sections at least 50 by 50 centimeters on primed walls. Live with them for 48 hours to see how the color reacts to sunlight and artificial light.

Connect trims and accents

Even with a striking accent wall, trims should feel unified. We typically keep doors and ceilings in the same neutral to maintain a calm transition throughout the home.
